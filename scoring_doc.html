1. Point Distribution & Multiplier Scheme

Scoring Category Points Requirement / Rule
Participation Base (Initiation Grant) 50 PTS Guaranteed baseline for all participants upon entering the grid quest.
Standard MCQ Correct Answer +2 PTS Awarded on every correct answer. 5 questions per domain × 2 pts = 10 pts/domain.
Speed Efficiency Bonus +1 PT Earned if question is solved within the 10-second timer window.
Streak Continuity Bonus +1 PT Awarded on consecutive correct answers (Active Streak ≥ 2).
Domain Mastery Clearance +1 PT Awarded when all 5 questions in a domain portal are solved.
Incorrect / Failed Answer 0 PTS No negative penalty. Streak multiplier resets to 0.
Replay Mode (Already Solved) 0 PTS Anti-farming protection: Re-entering a solved portal awards no duplicate marks.
Absolute Maximum Cap 250 PTS Total quest score cannot exceed 250 PTS under any combination of multipliers.
